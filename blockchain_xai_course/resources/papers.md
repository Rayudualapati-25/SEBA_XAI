## Reading workflow recommendation

1. Read **one foundational** paper from each track per week.
2. Skim **three frontier** papers per week (read abstract, intro, conclusion, figures).
3. Maintain a **paper-card** for each (Zettelkasten style):
   - Problem
   - Method (one paragraph)
   - Result (key numbers)
   - Limitation
   - One follow-up question
4. After 3 months you will have ~50 cards — enough for a meaningful research-direction decision.
