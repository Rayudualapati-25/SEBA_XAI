# Hands-On Labs

Six structured labs that take you from beginner to research-ready. Each lab targets ~4–8 hours of work and has a concrete deliverable.

| # | Title | Tracks | Time | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build a toy blockchain in Python | Blockchain | 4 hrs | Working 5-block chain with Merkle proofs |
| 2 | Reentrancy attack reproduction | Blockchain / security | 3 hrs | Foundry exploit + fix |
| 3 | Your first ZK circuit | Blockchain / ZK | 4 hrs | Verified Sepolia tx |
| 4 | SHAP from scratch | XAI | 4 hrs | KernelSHAP implementation + validation |
| 5 | Saliency methods + sanity checks | XAI | 4 hrs | Table comparing 4 methods |
| 6 | zkML prototype | Intersection | 8 hrs | EZKL proof of small NN inference |

See `lab_1.md` … `lab_6.md` for full instructions.

---

## Lab philosophy

Each lab follows the **plan → build → verify → write** loop:

1. **Plan** — write down what you'll do in 5 lines before opening an editor.
2. **Build** — minimum viable code.
3. **Verify** — explicit test or assertion that the lab worked.
4. **Write** — a half-page journal entry: what surprised you, what you'd improve.

The writing step is where you build the research intuition. Don't skip it.
