# XAI Track — Overview

A 13-module sequence covering Explainable AI from foundations to the 2026 research frontier.

## Modules

1. [Foundations: What Is Explainability?](01_foundations.md)
2. [Taxonomy of XAI Methods](02_taxonomy.md)
3. [LIME, SHAP & Feature Attribution](03_feature_attribution.md)
4. [Gradient-Based Attribution](04_gradient_methods.md)
5. [Concept-Based Explanations](05_concept_based.md) — TCAV, ACE, CRAFT
6. [Counterfactual & Contrastive Explanations](06_counterfactuals.md)
7. [Global Methods](07_global_methods.md) — PDP, ICE, ALE, surrogates
8. [Attention & Transformer Interpretability](08_attention_transformers.md)
9. [Mechanistic Interpretability](09_mechanistic_interpretability.md)
10. [Evaluating Explanations](10_evaluation_metrics.md)
11. [Human-Centered XAI](11_human_centered.md)
12. [Domain Applications](12_applications.md) — medicine, finance, law, NLP
13. [Research Frontiers (2025–2027)](13_research_frontiers.md)

## How to study this track

- Read in order. Modules 1–2 are conceptual; 3–9 are method-focused; 10–13 are evaluation and frontier.
- Do all six labs from `../labs/`.
- After every module, add 5–10 paper-cards (Zettelkasten-style) to your research notes.

## What you will be able to do after

- Pick the right XAI method for any model + audience combination.
- Implement LIME, SHAP, IG, Grad-CAM, TCAV, and a counterfactual generator from scratch.
- Reproduce mechanistic-interpretability case studies (induction heads, IOI) on small models.
- Critically evaluate XAI papers — spot unfaithful methods, missing sanity checks, weak baselines.
- Identify a tractable open problem and write a workshop-paper-scale proposal.

- Casual (6 hrs/week): 24 weeks
