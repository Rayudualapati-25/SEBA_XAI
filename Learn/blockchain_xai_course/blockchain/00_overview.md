# Blockchain Track — Overview

A 12-module sequence from cryptographic primitives to research-grade frontiers.

## Modules

1. [Cryptographic Foundations](01_foundations.md) — hashes, Merkle trees, signatures, commitments
2. [Distributed Systems & CAP/FLP](02_distributed_systems.md)
3. [Bitcoin Deep Dive](03_bitcoin.md)
4. [Ethereum & Smart Contracts](04_ethereum.md)
5. [Consensus Algorithms](05_consensus.md) — PoW, PoS, BFT, VRF families
6. [Scalability & Layer 2](06_scalability_layer2.md) — rollups, channels, DA
7. [Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Proofs](07_privacy_zk.md)
8. [DeFi Architecture](08_defi.md)
9. [Security & Attack Surface](09_security_attacks.md)
10. [Governance & Tokenomics](10_governance_tokenomics.md)
11. [Advanced Consensus (DAG, HotStuff, Sharding)](11_advanced_consensus.md)
12. [Research Frontiers (2025–2027)](12_research_frontiers.md)

## How to study this track

- Read modules in order. Each builds on the previous.
- Do the lab at the end of each module before moving on.
- After every 3 modules, write a 1-page comparison of what you learned (cross-module synthesis).

## What you will be able to do after

- Read a CRYPTO / S&P / OSDI blockchain paper without translation.
- Implement a toy blockchain, a smart contract, a fraud-proof bisection, and a Circom circuit.
- Critique any new "L2" or "L3" launch with informed skepticism.
- Identify a tractable research problem from one of the 12 frontier areas.

## Estimated time

- Casual (6 hrs/week): 24 weeks
- Focused (15 hrs/week): 10 weeks
- Full immersion (30 hrs/week): 5 weeks
